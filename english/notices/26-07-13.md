# 26-07-13

## AutoCAD Add-ins Are Now Live

Our AutoCAD add-ins and stability update are officially live, and scheduled maintenance is now complete. Thank you for your patience during the maintenance.

To get the update, close Revit and AutoCAD, then relaunch BIMIL. It will update to the latest version automatically. Log in to explore the new AutoCAD add-ins and the improved stability update.\
Detailed guides for each AutoCAD add-in are now available on [BIMIL Docs](../add-ins/autocad/).

***

### If you updated during the maintenance window

If you updated BIMIL or installed add-ins during the maintenance window (03:00 to 04:00 UTC / 12:00 to 13:00 KST), the installation may not have completed correctly. If you encounter errors when launching an add-in, please follow the steps below:



1. Open BIMIL
2. Go to Settings and select Remove BIMIL Add-ins
3. Reinstall the add-ins you need



This will resolve the issue in most cases. If the problem persists after reinstalling, please contact us and our support team will assist you.
